Sanket — System Architecture & Documentation

What is Sanket?
An offline-capable AI safety intelligence system engineered for Indian Coal Mining Environmental Engineering & Safety Audits. Backed by the official DGMS SANKET (2016–2022) report, Coal Mines Regulations (CMR 2017), and 120+ years of historical disaster archives (1901–2022).

The 6 Core Safety Intelligence Pillars

1. Worker Transfer Advisories

Role-specific hazard alerts (drillers, blasters, loaders) and pre-shift statutory checklists across 42+ collieries.

2. Engineer Revisit Plans

Statutory 5-phase re-entry protocols for reopening sealed districts, recovering headings, and ventilation restoration.

3. Historical Disaster Dossiers

Deep-dives into Dhori (1965, 268 dead), Chinakuri (176 dead), Bhatdee (50 dead), Poidih (209 dead), and inquiry commissions.

4. 4-Pillar Prevention Blueprint

CMR 153 Methane elimination, CMR 136 Stone dusting & barriers, CMR 183/204 Ignition controls, and CMR 170 Telemetry.

5. DGMS Institutional Mandates

Sections 22/22A prohibition powers, Section 24 Courts of Inquiry, Board of Mining Examinations, and Safety Circulars.

6. Explosion Thermodynamics

Coward flammability diagram, LEL/UEL, Graham's ratio, MEC (30-45 g/m³), and stone dust heat absorption kinetics.
